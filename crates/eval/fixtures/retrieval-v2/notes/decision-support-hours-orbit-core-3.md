# Decision revised: support hours for Orbit Core

Orbit Core switches its support hours to 9am to 5pm. This replaces the earlier choice on support hours, and the change takes effect on 26 July 2025. We made the call after looking at when requests actually arrive and when people are around to answer them. Anyone with a rota or calendar entry tied to the old hours should adjust it before that date, and questions can go to the team.
