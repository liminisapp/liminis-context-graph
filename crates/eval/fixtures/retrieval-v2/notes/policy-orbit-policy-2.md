# Orbit Policy — what we know

Orbit Policy is the policy responsible for security, and people tend to ask about it at awkward moments. The review cadence for the Orbit Policy is monthly, so it never sits untouched for long, and any change that someone proposes is likely to get looked at promptly instead of languishing.
