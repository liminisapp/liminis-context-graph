# Notes on Marlin Policy

Putting this here so I stop answering the same question in chat. Marlin Policy is the policy responsible for onboarding, and it governs how newcomers' requests are handled. Under the Marlin Policy, the submission window is 26 days, so anything later than that needs a good reason. The Marlin Policy exception approver is Callum Brennan, and he is the person to ask if a request falls outside the window. I will add examples of past exceptions when I have gathered a few, since people tend to understand rules better with a case attached.
