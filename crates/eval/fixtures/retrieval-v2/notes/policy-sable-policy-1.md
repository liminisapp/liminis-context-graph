# Sable Policy: working notes

Sable Policy is the policy that governs scheduling, and people keep asking me the same two questions about it, so here are the answers. Under the Sable Policy, the submission window is 55 days, so plan requests accordingly and do not leave them to the end. Exceptions to the Sable Policy need sign-off from Bram Tanaka, and he would prefer to hear about them before the deadline rather than after. That is all for now. I will update this note if anything changes.
