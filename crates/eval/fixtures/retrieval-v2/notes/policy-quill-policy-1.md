# Quill Policy: working notes

Writing down what I understand about the Quill Policy, which is the policy responsible for inventory, because people keep asking me and I keep answering from memory. The Quill Policy allows 6 days to submit a request, and that clock is stricter than most people assume. The Quill Policy exception approver is Bram Tanaka, so if a request misses the window, he is the person to ask. I would rather ask early than explain afterwards.
