## Incidents

The quarterly incident tally is now complete, and the meeting spent a good half hour on it. Sable Core logged 36 incidents during the quarter. The compliance side tends to log generously, because anything that touches a rule is worth writing down, even if it turns out to be minor. That is deliberate, and nobody wants it to change, but it does make raw counts hard to compare across efforts. The more useful reading came from the notes attached to each entry: most were paperwork gaps that were fixed within a day, and a small number pointed to a process that is genuinely awkward. Those are going onto a short list for redesign.

On the inventory side, Lena Moreau logged 4 incidents during the quarter. She was quick to say that the low figure should not be taken as proof that everything is perfect, since a few near-misses never made it into the log and she intends to start capturing them. Her view is that a log with only dramatic events teaches you very little, and the quiet ones are where the patterns show up. The group supported that and agreed to treat near-misses as worth a one-line entry.

There was a thoughtful exchange about whether counts should be compared at all. The consensus: use them to look for change over time within one effort, and avoid ranking efforts against each other. A rise may mean more problems or better reporting, and only the notes can tell you which.

## Offsite

Planning for the Orbit Core offsite is underway, and the Orbit Core offsite will take place in Porto. The conversation so far has been about shape rather than detail. The inventory team would like a balance of working sessions and open time, with one session devoted to how stock information flows between systems, because that is where most of the friction has been this year. People also asked for a session on how we write things down, which has become a theme across the group.

Practical questions are being handled step by step. Someone in finance will check what the approvals process needs, and the team will confirm availability before anything is booked. A few people mentioned they would prefer to avoid travelling on the same day as the first session, so the plan is to arrive the evening before. Nobody wants to start a working day tired from a journey.

It sounds minor, but it has already helped: two people found and fixed outdated entries within a day of the change. The next step is a light review rota, so pages do not drift again.

Other housekeeping items:

- The shared mailbox filters have been tidied, and automated notices now land in a separate folder.
- The old documents folder has been archived with a clear label.

## Decisions

A few small decisions were recorded this week. Near-misses will get a one-line entry in the incident log. Stock pages will carry a named owner and a last-reviewed line. Comparisons of incident counts will be used for trends within one effort and not for ranking. The offsite planning will continue with availability checks first.
