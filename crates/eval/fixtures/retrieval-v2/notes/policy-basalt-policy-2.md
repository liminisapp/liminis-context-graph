# Basalt Policy — what we know

Basalt Policy is the policy that governs billing, and it comes up more often than you would think. Basalt Policy gets a annually review, which means there is only one chance a year to fix anything in it, so it is worth keeping a running list of complaints and awkward cases for whenever that comes around. I wrote this down because three people asked me about it this week and I had to guess each time. Also, the kettle in the small kitchen is broken again, in case anyone was wondering about the tea.
