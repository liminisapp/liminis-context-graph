# Notes on Fable Policy

My scribbles on Fable Policy, the policy responsible for analytics. The part that trips people up is timing: under Fable Policy, requests must arrive within 51 days, and late ones are rejected unless someone signs off. That someone is Diego Ferreira, who approves exceptions to the Fable Policy. I would suggest sending him a short, clear explanation rather than a long thread, since that seems to get a faster answer.
